Explore Our Work

Our Core Objectives

🔬

Medical Research

  • Conduct clinical & translational research in underserved regions.
  • Develop innovative low-cost healthcare solutions.
  • Collaborate with academic institutions.
🩺

Patient Support

  • Financial & logistical support for underprivileged patients.
  • Organize free medical camps and screenings.
  • Community awareness programs.
🎓

Education

  • Workshops, seminars, and conferences for professionals.
  • Scholarships & fellowships for specialized training.
  • Public health education materials.

How We Are Organized

The Sharda Foundation is governed by a dedicated Managing Committee responsible for day-to-day operations and decision-making. We believe in transparency and community involvement.

Managing Committee Structure:

  • • President
  • • Secretary
  • • Treasurer
  • • Executive Members (3)

* Elections are held every 5 years. Accounts are audited annually.
